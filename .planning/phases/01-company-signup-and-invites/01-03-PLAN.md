## Phase Goal

**As a** first user of a client company, **I want to** create my company at signup and invite teammates by email, **so that** later people join only that company and Google cannot drop anyone into a global employee pool.

<objective>
Stop Google from inserting unknown users into a global pool; allow Google create-company only with a pending company cookie; show Invite required; restore sign-in footer Create a company.

Purpose: TENANT-05 plus Google TENANT-01 path. Invite-cookie join is 01-05.
Output: pending-context route, auth.ts gate, error branch, sign-in/signup Google behavior.
</objective>
