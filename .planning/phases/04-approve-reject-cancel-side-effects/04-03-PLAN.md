## Phase Goal

**As a** manager or employee, **I want to** approve, reject, or cancel a single leave request so remaining days deduct or restore, a durable audit row uses my real user id, and the employee is notified in-app and by email, **so that** the request lifecycle is truthful on a shared deployment.
